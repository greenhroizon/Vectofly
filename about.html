About VectoFly

VectoFly is a free endless 3D plane game that runs right in your browser, anywhere in the world. Pick a plane, dodge the red spikes, grab blue repair pills and orange fuel cans, and see how high you can climb on the global leaderboard.

Play VectoFly

Who we are

VectoFly is made and operated by a small team that loves simple, fast browser games. We keep the Game free, light and easy to play on desktop, tablet and mobile.

How it is built

The Game uses HTML5, JavaScript and WebGL (Three.js). It is based on "The Aviator" by Karim Maaloul (Codrops), which we have extended with new planes, paints, maps, difficulty levels, a hangar and a global leaderboard.
