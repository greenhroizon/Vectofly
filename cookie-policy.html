Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how VectoFly uses cookies and similar technologies such as browser local storage, for players in every country.

What we use

  • Local storage (strictly necessary): saves your settings, coins, unlocked items, difficulty and personal best scores on your device so the Game works. This does not require consent under rules such as the EU ePrivacy Directive because it is needed to provide the service you asked for.
  • Third-party scripts: the Game loads the CrazyGames SDK and may load other third-party services. They can set their own cookies or storage under their own policies.
  • Analytics and advertising (optional): not used at the time of writing. If we add tools such as Google Analytics or Google AdSense, they will use cookies for statistics and for showing ads. For visitors in the EEA, UK and other regions that require it, we will only load them after you give consent through a cookie banner, and you will be able to change your choice at any time.

Managing cookies and storage

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you clear local storage, saved coins, unlocks and personal bests on this device will be lost. To opt out of personalised ads: Google Ads Settings, aboutads.info (US), youronlinechoices.com (EU) or youradchoices.ca (Canada).
