What you can contact us about

  • Bug reports: include your country, device, browser and what happened.
  • Feedback and ideas: new planes, maps or features you would like.
  • Privacy requests (GDPR, CCPA and others): use the subject "Privacy request" and see our Privacy Policy.
  • Leaderboard removal: send your pilot name and approximate score.
  • Copyright notices: see our copyright page.
  • Business and partnerships: use the subject "Business".

Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information by email.
