Copyright Notice and DMCA

Last updated: October 29, 2026

We respect intellectual property rights worldwide. If you believe that content on this website infringes your copyright, send us a notice and we will review it promptly. This process follows the US DMCA and works for notices under the copyright laws of other countries too, including the EU Digital Services Act notice rules.

What to include in your notice

  1. Your name and contact details (an email address is enough).
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed.
  3. The exact URL or location on this website of the material you want removed.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act for them.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is fine).

Where to send it

Email itsvasudev404@gmail.com with the subject line "Copyright notice". If the claim is valid, we will remove or disable access to the material. We may share your notice with the person who posted the material.

Counter notice

If you believe material was removed by mistake, email us with the URL and an explanation, and we will review it. We may restore it if the notice is withdrawn or the claim is not upheld.

Repeat infringers

We may restrict access for people who repeatedly infringe copyright.
